Other Tools

  • Cluely

    • Cluely uses your screen and audio to provide intelligence during meetings, sales calls, and everything on your computer.

  • Genspark Super Agent

    The Genspark Super Agent is an autonomous, no-code AI assistant that uses a "Mixture-of-Agents" architecture to execute complex, multi-step real-world tasks.
    Key Capabilities & Use Cases:
    • Real-World Actions: It can make real phone calls for tasks like booking reservations or scheduling appointments.
    • Content Creation: Instantly generates professional presentations (AI Slides), videos, and websites.
    • Research & Planning: Conducts in-depth, real-time research, synthesizes data, and autonomously plans entire projects, such as travel itineraries.
    • Productivity: Automates multi-step workflows with simple prompts, effectively acting as an all-in-one digital assistant.

  • meet-ting

    An AI email scheduling assistant that books meetings directly from your inbox - no apps, no downloads, as easy as sending an email.

  • Mixboard (Google Labs)

    • What it does: Mixboard is an AI-powered concepting board that allows you to explore, expand, and refine visual and textual ideas on an open canvas.
    • Typical Use Cases & Examples:
      • Brainstorming & Mood Boards: Quickly generating visual concepts for a project (e.g., a "mid-century modern coastal living room").
      • Brand Mockups: Uploading a logo and using AI prompts to instantly see it on different mockups (e.g., "Change the color of the shirt to black").
      • Event Planning: Generating complete visual concept boards from a simple theme (e.g., "rustic autumn wedding").
    • Best Practices:
      • Use it for Ideation, Not Final Assets: Treat it as a creative starting point, as the outputs are not intended for high-resolution, production-ready use.
      • Iterate Quickly: Leverage the "regenerate" and "more like this" features to explore a wide range of variations in minutes.
      • Refine with Natural Language: Use simple, conversational commands to make edits and remix concepts directly on the canvas.

  • Pomelli (Google Labs)

    • What it does: Pomelli is an AI marketing tool that automatically builds brand-consistent campaign ideas and assets by analyzing a business's website.
    • Typical Use Cases & Examples:
      • On-Brand Content Creation: Generating social media posts, display banners, and ad copy that instantly matches your existing brand's tone, color palette, and style.
      • Campaign Ideation: Getting tailored campaign suggestions based on your "Business DNA" (e.g., "create a summer sale campaign" for an e-commerce brand).
      • Maintaining Consistency: Ensuring that all assets created across different channels (social, ads, email) look and sound cohesive.
    • Best Practices:
      • Start with a Clear Foundation: Ensure your website accurately reflects your current, desired brand identity, as this is the primary source Pomelli learns from.
      • Treat AI as a Collaborator: Always review and human-polish the AI-generated content before publication to ensure authenticity and accuracy.
      • Test and Measure: Establish metrics to compare the performance (time savings, consistency scores, engagement) of AI-generated content versus manually created assets.

  • Opal (Google Labs)

    • What it does: Opal is a no-code platform that lets you build, edit, and share custom AI mini-applications and multi-step workflows using natural language.
    • Typical Use Cases & Examples:
      • Automated Content Pipelines: Creating a tool that generates a 1000-word blog post, captions, and video scripts from a single product idea.
      • Business Process Automation: Building a system that analyzes sales data from a spreadsheet and generates a trend report with charts.
      • Creating Internal Tools: Developing a chatbot that answers questions about a company's return policy or a tool for summarizing complex legal documents.
    • Best Practices:
      • Write Effective, Structured Prompts: Use a clear structure (context, constraints, desired output format like JSON) for better and more predictable results.
      • Break Down Complexity: For intricate tasks, break them into smaller, sequential steps or "chain prompts" to manage the workflow.
      • Leverage Both Modes: Start with natural language for rapid prototyping, then switch to the visual editor for fine-tuning complex logic and seeing the data flow.

  • Stitch (Google Labs)

    • Stitch is an AI-powered tool to help application builders generate high-quality user interfaces for mobile and web apps, and easily export them to Figma, or directly access the frontend code.  It uses some of the latest AI models from Google DeepMind.
    • Google Stitch + Claude Code = Insane App Design

  • Genie 3 (Google DeepMind)

    • Genie 3 is a general-purpose world model. It uses simple text descriptions to generate photorealistic environments that can be explored in real-time.

  • Disco (Google Labs)

    • Transforms messy, multi-tab browsing sessions into structured, interactive "mini-apps" called GenTabs. It enables users to instantly organize, compare, and act on web data—such as planning trips, managing projects, or comparing products—directly within the browser, effectively turning research into functional applications without coding.
      Key Features and Capabilities:
      • GenTabs (Generative Tabs): The core functionality that analyzes open web pages and creates a customized, interactive interface (e.g., a dashboard with maps, calendars, and checklists).
      • Gemini 3 Integration: Uses the Gemini 3 model to understand, summarize, and act on content from multiple tabs simultaneously.
      • Task-Oriented Workflow: Shifts browsing from simply reading pages to taking action, such as building a 7-day meal plan or a, vacation itinerary, using data from the web.
      • Privacy-Focused: The tool operates on the provided tabs and chat data to generate results, aiming to keep user data within the context of their active session.
